# Thesis presentations and defense

Read applicable project instructions and `THESIS.md` if present, resolving paths
from that file. Establish audience, duration, purpose, required format, available
results, and the requested deliverable. Follow the existing presentation design
system and template. Do not assume access to a slide application.

Build the talk around the problem, research question, essential idea, strongest
evidence, contribution, and limitations. Orient listeners before specialized
terms. Give unfamiliar central ideas room across slides rather than compressing
an entire chapter onto one slide. Allocate time explicitly and check it by
rehearsal; a slide-per-minute estimate is only a starting point.

Give each slide one communicative purpose and a title that makes it clear.
Use a visual when it explains relationships or evidence better than prose.
Prefer short phrases and speaker notes to paragraphs on slides. A visual overview
often gives more orientation than a generic table of contents. Use animation
only to clarify a relationship or control information load.

For experimental slides, make the question, comparison, main finding, and
limitation visible. Use verified artifacts and retain axes, units, legends,
sample sizes or uncertainty where needed. Never invent an attractive result
curve. Check figures and text at presentation size, and number slides for Q&A.

Distinguish what the author borrowed, adapted, implemented, and demonstrated.
Put detailed derivations or settings in backup slides when appropriate. Prepare
answers about assumptions, baselines, alternative explanations, generalization,
and failed approaches. Say when evidence does not answer a question; do not
manufacture an experiment or hide a known flaw.

Return the requested outline, slide text, notes, visual briefs, or feedback.
If creating a deck is authorized and tools are available, use the project format
and inspect the rendered slides. Otherwise make the content usable in the
author's chosen tool and state that no deck was rendered.
